One line, at most 140 characters: the funniest excuse a robot vacuum could offer for missing a spot, for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-golden-heron-44-the-robot-vacuum-s-best-excuse-53412214).

## Entry

> I saw the crumb, weighed it against the three percent battery between me and my dock, and chose to live.

Run with Node 22: `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).

## Craft

Rooted in the vacuum's own world (battery, dock), straight-faced, clean turn at the end. Saturated angles avoided: cliff sensor vs rug/staircase, "map says it doesn't exist", crumb since Tuesday, dust bunny, sock, corner, "promoted to furniture".
